## 6. Notes for App Review

This field is doing real work for you. An eight-mode AI app with generated images, generated video, *and* consumable credits is exactly the shape that gets a 4.3 "AI wrapper" or 5.1.2(i) data-sharing rejection. Paste this verbatim.

```
WHAT THE APP DOES
Inkwoven is a handwriting-first AI notebook for iPad. The user writes on a PencilKit canvas with Apple Pencil or a finger. After roughly 3 seconds of pen rest, the app snapshots the region of new strokes and sends it to our serverless proxy, which routes it to a vision LLM. The reply is rendered back on the page as animated handwriting, as a generated image that develops in place, or (credit-gated) as a short generated video. There is no keyboard input on iPad by design.

WHY THIS IS NOT AN AI WRAPPER (Guideline 4.3)
The original functionality is the paper engine, not the model: idle-send state machine with speculative upload and stroke-cancel, region-diff snapshotting, a streamed cursive renderer that draws replies as Core Text glyph paths in a per-Book hand, a darkroom-style progressive image developer, and an exchange lifecycle that archives the user's strokes off the live canvas. The eight "Books" are content modules with distinct prompts, hands, inks, papers, and modality policies running on that one engine. None of this exists in a chat client.

AI DISCLOSURE AND CONSENT (Guideline 5.1.2(i))
On first launch, before any page is sent, the user is shown an explicit, non-dismissible-by-default consent screen naming that page contents are transmitted to third-party AI providers for processing, and identifying them. Consent is revocable in Settings, which disables sending. This is not buried in the EULA. Per EU AI Act Article 50 (applicable 2 August 2026), every Book states that the user is interacting with AI at first interaction, and all generated images and video carry machine-readable provenance metadata.

SAFETY
A crisis classifier runs in parallel on every Book and every message. On a positive signal the Book breaks character, responds with care, and surfaces real resources; this includes Tutor frustration and dark Game Master scenarios. Both the prompt and the output of every image and video request pass moderation, with the strictest thresholds on video. Styles are deliberately illustrative; no photorealistic depictions of real people are generated. The Correspondent answers only as public-domain or original figures — no trademarked or living-person impersonation.

THE ORACLE (Guideline 4.3(b))
The Oracle is one of eight modes inside a broader notebook product, not a fortune-telling app. It answers questions and poses riddles in ink. It makes no predictive, divinatory, or supernatural claims.

PURCHASES
Inkwoven Plus is an auto-renewing subscription ($9.99/month, $59.99/year with a 7-day trial on annual) via RevenueCat over StoreKit 2. Moving-picture credits are consumables sold in packs of 10/30/100; a credit is refunded to the wallet automatically if generation fails. The Bindery sells non-consumable cosmetics. Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. The paywall is never shown during onboarding — only after a value moment.

ACCOUNT
Sign in with Apple is optional; the app is fully usable signed out. In-app account deletion and full export/delete-all are in Settings.

TESTING NOTES
No demo account is required. To reach the paywall quickly, use six magic moments in one day, or open a page older than 30 days. Every Book and every modality is behind a server-side kill-switch flag; if the reviewer sees a failing modality, we can disable it without a resubmission — please contact us before rejecting on that basis.

Contact for review questions: <your support email>
```

## 11. Two compliance items that are not ASO but will bite you

**EU AI Act, Article 50 — applies 2 August 2026.** That is tomorrow. Apps placed on the market from that date get **no grace period** (the December 2026 extension only covers systems already live). Two obligations touch you directly: every Book must make clear at *first interaction* that the user is talking to AI — a mention in the ToS is explicitly named as insufficient — and every generated image and video must carry machine-readable provenance marking (C2PA content credentials are the accepted route). The description and review notes above already state both; make sure the app actually does them. Penalties run to €15M or 3% of global turnover.

**Guideline 5.1.2(i), tightened November 2025.** Explicit opt-in before page contents reach a third-party AI service, disclosed in-app and in the privacy policy — not buried in the EULA.
