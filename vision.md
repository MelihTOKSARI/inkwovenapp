# Inkwoven — Product Vision

**One-liner:** An enchanted notebook for iPad — write with your Pencil, the page drinks your ink, and it answers in flowing script, a developing picture, or a **moving photograph you can fall into**. Paper that answers.
**Status:** Vision v2 (platform) · MVP stage
**Last updated:** 2026-07-06

---

## The concept (source of truth)

Inkwoven is **a medium, not a single-purpose app**. The product is the *paper engine* — handwriting in, magic out in three grammars: **ink** (a reply written back in a flowing hand), **pictures** (an image that develops on the page like a photograph in a tray), and **moving pictures** (short living images, Daily-Prophet style). What the paper is *for* is decided by which **Book** you open. Books are content modules on one shared engine — the journal is one book on the shelf, not the identity of the product.

This is the anti-chatbot: every AI product is a cold chat window with a keyboard; Inkwoven is an enchanted object with a pen. That contrast is the brand, the demo, and the moat.

**The moving picture is the hero, not the garnish.** The Tom Riddle diary; the Daily Prophet. A page that writes back is lovely — a page that becomes a window you fall into is the thing people screen-record and send to a friend. When a reply is a scene, the page offers to make it move; tap the clip and it expands past the page to fill the screen, looping, no chrome. **This is launch scope and the launch does not happen without it.** Every other modality supports it.

The conversion is always **user-triggered**. The model judges whether a reply is convertible — a scene has visual life, a worked equation does not — and only then does the affordance appear. Nobody is charged for a clip they didn't ask for, and the page never spends money on the user's behalf.

## The opportunity

Three proven demand pools converge on one engine:
- **AI reflection/journaling** — Rosebud et al. prove ~$10/mo willingness-to-pay; all typing-first.
- **AI creativity** — doodle-to-art and co-writing mechanics are among the most-shared AI content formats; none are pen-native.
- **Solo play** — solo RPG/interactive-fiction communities are underserved, passionate, and high-WTP.

No competitor serves any of these through handwriting, and none can pivot to it without a ground-up rebuild. **Why now:** vision LLMs read handwriting straight off a canvas; image generation is fast/cheap enough to feel instant; the open-source "Riddle" project proved organic fascination with the mechanic; iPad + Pencil installed base keeps growing.

## Positioning statement

> For iPad + Apple Pencil owners who find AI chatbots cold and single-purpose apps limiting,
> **Inkwoven** is the enchanted notebook whose pages answer your handwriting — with ink, pictures, or moving pictures — and become whatever you need: a diary, a storyteller, a game master, an artist, an oracle,
> unlike ChatGPT/Rosebud/GoodNotes, which are keyboards, single verticals, or dumb paper.

## The hard truth we hold ourselves to (launch discipline)

**Broad concept ≠ unfocused launch.** "It does everything" products die at acquisition because nobody can repeat what they're for. The rule:
1. **The brand is the medium** — "paper that answers" — one sentence, one gasp-inducing demo.
2. **We launch with exactly 5 sharp Books**, each with its own 20-second demo video aimed at its own audience. Same app, five doors in.
3. **Every later Book is a content drop and a marketing event** (own press cycle, own video, own audience), never launch-scope creep.
4. Any feature that can't be traced to the engine or one of the 5 launch Books is Future. No exceptions without a PRD revision.

## The launch shelf (5 Books) and the first drops

| Book | What the paper does | Audience / hook |
|---|---|---|
| **The Storyteller** | Continues your tale in ink; scenes develop as paintings on the page | Writers, parents (bedtime stories that draw themselves) |
| **The Artist** | Your doodle develops into finished art in a chosen style | The most shareable mechanic; everyone |
| **The Game Master** | Runs a solo adventure: write actions, it narrates; illustrated moments | Solo RPG / interactive fiction niche, high WTP |
| **The Oracle** | Cryptic or plain answers, riddles, daily card drawn in ink | Viral candy; zero-onboarding first-session magic |

**First content drops (post-launch, in order):** The Correspondent (letters answered by historical/fictional hands), The Tutor (worked solutions and corrections in ink — education is its own business), Parlor Games (riddles, 20 questions, draw-and-guess), seasonal editions (Halloween grimoire, travel log). **Later platform bet:** creator-published Books with rev-share.

## Target user

**Best-fit segment:** 18–45, owns iPad + Pencil (an affluent, design-sensitive, creativity-skewed base), already AI-curious, drawn to cozy-magic aesthetics.
**Switching trigger:** seeing the demo — ink drunk by the page and an answer flowing back, a doodle developing into art, or a written scene blooming into a moving picture that fills the screen. The product moment *is* the ad. Secondary triggers per Book: "I should journal again," "I want a solo campaign," "my kid wants a bedtime story."

**Our wedge:** the only pen-first AI medium — one enchanted object that answers in ink, pictures, and **moving pictures you can step inside**, with a shelf of Books no chat window can imitate. The handwriting is how you get in; the moving picture is why you stay and why anyone else hears about it.

## Value proposition

**Jobs:** create (stories, art), play (adventures, games), reflect (diary), learn (later), delight & share (functional/emotional/social).
**Pains:** chatbots feel like work software; creative tools have blank-canvas paralysis; journaling dies unanswered; solo play lacks a game master.
**Gains:** every page answers; the pen is the only interface; a magical object you *want* to open; a demo your friends make you repeat.

| Pain reliever / Gain creator | Fit |
|---|---|
| Paper engine: write → absorb → answer (ink/image) → the core gasp | ✅ |
| 5 Books → the right magic for the moment, one object | ✅ |
| **Moving pictures: a reply becomes a scene you fall into** | ✅ **the hero moment — launch-blocking** |

## North Star Metric

**NSM: weekly magic moments per active user** — pages answered in any Book, any modality (ink reply, developed image, moving picture). Use-case agnostic like the product; captures the habit loop across the shelf; leading for retention and conversion; actionable via Book drops, prompts, and reply quality.
Headline number users see (not the NSM): pages filled in their notebook ("your notebook grows").
Input metrics: D1 activation (first answered page), Books opened per user in week 1, image-reply rate, Book-drop adoption, share/screen-record events.

## Monetization

- **Free:** the notebook + all 8 Books, 5 magic moments/day (occasional image included), 30-day page archive. Enough to gasp; a ceiling you feel.
- **Inkwoven Plus — $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr:** unlimited ink replies, generous image allotment, full archive, cross-page memory, all Spirits/hands, unlimited notebooks.
- **Credits (consumable):** moving pictures sold in packs — video unit costs never bundled unlimited.
- **Content IAP:** Book packs, covers, inks, seasonal editions — high margin, zero AI cost.
- **Later:** creator marketplace rev-share; education licensing (Tutor).
- Target free→paid 4–6% by week 8. RevenueCat / StoreKit 2; 30%→15% small-business share.
